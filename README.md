# Astrology study cards

Mobile flashcards for Level 1 of the Debra Silverman Applied Astrology course. One page, four decks:

- Signs (12)
- Planets (10)
- Elements (4, plus the 6 blends)
- Modes (3)

Tap to flip, swipe or use Prev and Next to move, and tap a rail button to jump. The page remembers the last deck and card.

Content follows the Level 1 coursebook and the study guides in the Astrology Drive folder (Topics/signs-quick-reference.md and Topics/major-configurations-and-oppositions.md). When a card and a study guide disagree, fix both.
